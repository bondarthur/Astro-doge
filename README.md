# ASTRO DODGE

A minimal retro 90's-style pixel web game. Open `index.html` in any browser — no build, no dependencies.

## How to play
- **Arrow keys**: move the astronaut (laptop/desktop)
- **Touch**: drag anywhere to move — the astronaut floats above your finger, and the lift automatically grows with your finger's contact size, so thumbs and angled fingers never hide it (phones/tablets); tap to start/retry
- **Enter / Space**: start or retry
- **M / speaker icon** (top-right): toggle all audio

## Dodge
Asteroids, planets, comets, UFOs and satellites — the longer you survive, the more spawn and the faster they come:

- **0–2:30 — Phase 1: DRIFT** (easy)
- **2:30–4:00 — Phase 2: STORM** (challenging)
- **4:00+ — Phase 3: CHAOS** (hardcore)

Survival time earns points continuously, plus **+1,000,000 bonus every 1:30 survived**. Best score saved locally.

## Collect (green glow = good)
Fly *into* glowing objects to grab them — they vanish in a burst of points:

- **Space gems** (regular): **+200,000** each
- **Rare visitors** — lost astronaut, space squirrel, space giraffe, rubber duck, magic mushroom, space pizza (~1 in 2 plays): **+600,000**
- **Legendaries** — Tesla roadster, ISS, Eiffel Tower, alien (~1 in 20 plays): **+2,000,000**

## Music
Procedural chiptune that evolves with the phase: dreamy space-pad in DRIFT, driving square-wave bassline in STORM, fast epic saw arpeggios in CHAOS.
